Overview

Engagement tools nobody opened.

HERE wanted employees to take part in learning, surveys and recognition, but traditional intranet tools felt like chores. Empower turns participation into a game with a clear goal: finish the race.

I led the design from research to visual design: the race metaphor, the home feed, quizzes, polls, events, stories, the personal journey, rewards and a manager dashboard.

The challenge

Make participation feel like play.

Engagement only works if people come back. The platform needed a reason to return every week, without feeling childish in a global tech company.

A

Scattered activities

Quizzes, surveys, events and trainings lived in separate tools.

B

No visible progress

Taking part earned nothing anyone could see.

C

Recognition was private

Wins and appreciation rarely reached the wider team.

D

Managers flew blind

No simple view of team engagement or mood.

Research

Why a race?

Research looked at motorsport broadcasts, power rankings and gamified products to find a metaphor that is competitive, team-based and familiar to a global workforce.

Laps, not levels

Progress shown as laps and points around a circuit everyone understands.

Team over individual

Leaderboards by team, department and location, not just people.

Every action counts

Quizzes, polls, events and stories all earn coins.

Rewards worth wanting

A store of racing gear and vouchers to spend coins on.

A home page that feels like race day

Home shows your lap, points and next actions, a feed of team wins, the weekly progress chart and trainings. The leaderboard ranks drivers by points with team colours.

Decision

Your lap and points are the first thing you see, every visit.

Every activity has its own space, but they share one card pattern: what it is, when it ends, and one clear action.

What I learned

A strong metaphor does half the work. The race theme explained progress, rank and rewards without a tutorial.

Celebrate in public. Visible wins drove more participation than private points.

Give managers a reason to care. The dashboard turned engagement data into team conversations.
